Eligibility and Required Documents

Basic Eligibility

  • Must be enrolled in an educational institution.
  • Must meet the scholarship's academic criteria.
  • Must satisfy applicable family income criteria.
  • Must provide accurate application details.

Required Documents

  • Student identity proof
  • Previous academic marksheet
  • Income certificate, if applicable
  • Bonafide certificate, if required
